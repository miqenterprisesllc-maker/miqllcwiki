## Implementation checklist

- Each important page has a unique `<title>`, meta description, one H1, clear H2/H3 structure, canonical URL, and relevant internal links.
- Service and blog pages include FAQ sections and schema where content genuinely answers questions.
- External references point to reliable sources (e.g., current Wikipedia policy pages) and are reviewed for accuracy.
- Avoid fabricated statistics, fake examples presented as fact, and misleading claims about Wikipedia outcomes.
